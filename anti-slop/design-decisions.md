# Padel Prime visual direction

Scope: homepage and public booking schedule. The user requested a modern, interactive improvement using the installed anti-slop skills, then authorized proceeding with the proposed sporty editorial direction.

Mode: apply anti-slop during implementation. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

- Charcoal and the existing lime accent retain Padel Prime's sports identity. Lime marks the main action and selected date; it is not applied to every icon or surface.
- A fixed dark brand canvas frames the photographic introduction, while warm off-white gives the court comparison a different reading rhythm. These are intentional section surfaces, not switchable themes.
- Large, tightly set Arial headings have the directness of sports posters. The locally available type stack avoids a remote font dependency; body copy uses a comfortable reading size.
- The hero introduces the club, the live date finder leads to booking, the court comparison helps choose indoor or outdoor, and booking details explain the actual hold/equipment/reschedule rules.
- The hero uses a verified padel-court photo, explicitly labeled as illustrative rather than a photo of this venue. Source: [Bruno Vaccaro Vercellino on Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-tennis-court-with-a-fence-surrounding-it-a4SslXtr1TE), available under the Unsplash License. The old image showed tennis. No invented testimonials, venue claims, amenities, contact details, or customer statistics are introduced.
- Numeric slot totals and starting prices come from the availability API. The frontend keeps the API's inclusive today-through-day-seven window.
- The two court descriptions use asymmetric composition instead of repeated feature cards. Slot cards remain uniform because users need to compare equivalent times and prices.
- Existing navigation destinations are retained in a smaller navigation structure. Nonfunctional member form and placeholder links are removed from the homepage.
- Calendar and clock icons identify dates and duration. Menu and close icons identify navigation controls. Arrows indicate entering the booking flow or selecting a slot. Icons are supplementary to text.
- Shadows, glows, looping decorative animations, and glass surfaces are absent. The photo scrim protects text contrast; its gradient has a readability purpose.
- Entry transitions establish the hero hierarchy, selected controls respond to input, and short result transitions show that filters changed the schedule. Reduced-motion users get no animation.
- Mobile uses stacked content, a labeled expandable menu, a four-column date grid, and two-column time slots. Tablet and desktop progressively expand when the content fits.
- Keyboard focus, labeled selects, pressed filter states, explicit slot statuses, loading messages, retry feedback, and empty results are part of the delivered interface.

The checkout, member portal, staff portal, and backend remain existing application surfaces; the visual delivery gate applies to the changed homepage and booking schedule.
